Discover the tangible outcomes of the ELECT project: modernized courses, state-of-the-art laboratories, publications, and capacity-building events.

Modernized Courses

New and updated curricula in energy-efficient lighting, integrating theoretical foundations with practical applications.

Bachelor's Programme

Comprehensive 4-year degree covering lighting design, energy auditing, smart controls, and sustainable technologies.

  • 12 new specialized modules
  • Hands-on laboratory sessions
  • Industry-integrated capstone project
Download syllabus

Diploma Programme

Two-year vocational programme focused on practical installation, maintenance, and troubleshooting of lighting systems.

  • 8 technical modules
  • Workplace attachment
  • Industry-recognized certification
Download syllabus

Short Courses & CPD

Continuing Professional Development modules for industry professionals and technicians.

  • LED Retrofit Design (5 days)
  • Smart Lighting Controls (3 days)
  • Energy Auditing (4 days)
View schedule

Established Laboratories

State-of-the-art teaching and research laboratories equipped with modern lighting technologies.

Copperbelt University Lighting Lab

Fully equipped laboratory featuring LED testing equipment, integrating sphere, goniophotometer, and smart lighting control systems.

Research Grade Student Capacity: 30

Mulungushi University Lighting Lab

Practical training facility with demonstration boards, lighting simulation software, and energy monitoring equipment.

Teaching Focus Student Capacity: 25

Workshops & Seminars

Capacity-building events conducted for academic staff, students, and industry professionals.

1

Train-the-Trainer Workshop

March 2026 Copperbelt University 35 participants

Intensive 5-day workshop equipping 35 university lecturers with modern pedagogical approaches and technical knowledge in energy-efficient lighting.

2

International Symposium on Energy Efficiency

September 2026 Lusaka, Zambia 120+ attendees

Two-day symposium featuring international experts from Aalto University and Hellenic Open University, with panel discussions and networking sessions.

3

Industry- Academia Dialogue Forum

February 2027 Mulungushi University 80 participants

Bridging the gap between industry needs and academic training through stakeholder engagement and curriculum feedback sessions.

4

Student Hands-on Training Bootcamp

August 2027 Both Partner Universities 60 students

Practical bootcamp focused on LED installation, lighting design software, and energy auditing techniques for final-year students.
